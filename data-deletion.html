Perfect CRM Data Deletion Instructions

Status: DRAFT — For review only; not published

Last updated: [CONFIRM publication date]

Perfect CRM is a multi-business WhatsApp CRM platform. Each business that uses it has its own workspace. This page explains how to delete information held in Perfect CRM and how to remove Perfect CRM's access to Meta (Facebook, WhatsApp and catalogue) assets. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.

Perfect CRM is operated by Jones Abraham P, a sole proprietor, at 3/160, Soosai Street, Karuthapillaiyoor, Tenkasi – 627418, Tamil Nadu, India.

1. If you are a customer of a business that uses Perfect CRM

Your information, such as your WhatsApp number, messages and orders, is held for the business you communicated with. That business decides how it is used.

  1. Ask the business first. It can delete your contact record in Perfect CRM. That also deletes your conversations, messages and orders in that business's workspace.
  2. Or email us at support@topinformer.in with the subject "Perfect CRM - Data Deletion Request". Include: - Your WhatsApp phone number. - The name of the business you contacted. - What you would like deleted.

We will pass your request to that business and help it respond.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, PINs or other sensitive information.

2. If you are a business using Perfect CRM

A. Delete individual customer records (self-service)

  • A signed-in user of the business can delete a contact.
  • This deletes that contact's conversations, messages and orders from the live database.
  • Reminders and service requests linked to the contact are kept but are no longer linked to it. Delete them separately if needed.

B. Disconnect your Meta catalogue (self-service)

  • An owner or admin can choose Disconnect on the Meta catalogue card.
  • This immediately erases the stored catalogue access token.
  • Products already synced stay in your workspace until you delete them.

C. Remove Perfect CRM's access in Meta

In Meta Business Settings or WhatsApp Manager, remove Perfect CRM from your business portfolio's connected apps or partners, or revoke the system-user or catalogue access you granted.

After this, Perfect CRM can no longer use the revoked access. Information already stored in Perfect CRM is not deleted automatically.

D. Delete your WhatsApp connection, user accounts or whole workspace (by request)

  • Self-service deletion of a workspace or a WhatsApp connection is not currently available.
  • The business owner should email support@topinformer.in with the subject "Perfect CRM - Workspace Deletion Request".
  • State the business name and what you want deleted: the WhatsApp connection and its stored token, specific user accounts, or the entire workspace with its contacts, messages, orders, products and settings.
  • We will verify that the request comes from the business's owner before acting.

3. If you are a business user

To close your own user account, ask your business's owner to remove you, or email support@topinformer.in. Security records of past sign-ins and actions may be kept as described in the Privacy Policy.

4. What happens after a request

  • Verification: We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and authority. We act on business-level deletions only on the instruction of the business's owner.
  • Timing: [CONFIRM response and completion timeframe with legal counsel before publication.]
  • Backups: Deleted information is removed from the live database first. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 14 days and in database point-in-time history for up to 6 hours, before being removed automatically.
  • What we may keep: We may retain information where the law requires it or where reasonably necessary for security or to establish or defend legal claims. We will explain if a request cannot be fully completed.
  • Meta's copies: Meta keeps its own copies of WhatsApp messages and catalogue data under its own policies. Requests for information held by Meta must be made to Meta.
