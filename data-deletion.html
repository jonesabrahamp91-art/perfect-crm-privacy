Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 26, 2026

About Perfect CRM

Perfect CRM is an internal customer relationship management tool used by Top Informer to manage customer conversations through one connected WhatsApp Business number.

How to request deletion

If you are a customer whose information may be held in Perfect CRM and you would like to request access to or deletion of that information, email support@topinformer.in.

Use the subject line Perfect CRM - Data Deletion Request. Briefly explain the request and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant conversation. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary sensitive information.

What happens next?

We will review the request and may ask for reasonable information to verify it. We will delete applicable personal information held in Perfect CRM unless retention is required by law or reasonably necessary for legitimate security or legal purposes. We will explain if a request cannot be fully completed because of such a requirement.

This request concerns information held by Perfect CRM. WhatsApp or Meta may separately retain or process information under their own policies and systems; requests to those services may need to be made directly to them.

Contact

For questions about data access or deletion, contact support@topinformer.in.
