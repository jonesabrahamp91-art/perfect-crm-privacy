The Business authorises Perfect CRM to access those assets for purposes such as sending and receiving WhatsApp messages, registering the number, subscribing to webhooks, reading and editing catalogue products, and linking the catalogue to WhatsApp.

The Business confirms that it owns, or is authorised to connect, every asset it connects.

The Business chooses and keeps the six-digit PIN for its WhatsApp number. Perfect CRM passes it to Meta and does not store it.

The Business can revoke access in Meta's settings. A connected catalogue can also be disconnected in Perfect CRM. Removing a WhatsApp connection from Perfect CRM currently requires a request to us; see the Data Deletion Instructions.
