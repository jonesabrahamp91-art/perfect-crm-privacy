1. Who we are

Perfect CRM is a multi-business customer relationship management (CRM) platform, offered as software-as-a-service, for businesses that communicate with their customers on WhatsApp. Each business that uses Perfect CRM gets its own separate workspace for its WhatsApp conversations, contacts, catalogue products and orders.

Perfect CRM is operated by Jones Abraham P, a sole proprietor, at:

This policy explains what information Perfect CRM processes, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and how to ask for access, correction or deletion.

2. Who this policy covers

  • Business users: owners, admins and agents of a business that uses Perfect CRM.
  • Business customers: people who message a business on WhatsApp, or whose details a business records in Perfect CRM ("customers").
  • Visitors: people who visit the Perfect CRM sign-in pages.

3. Our role and the role of each business

  • Customer data in a business's workspace: Each business decides why and how it uses its customers' information in Perfect CRM. The business is responsible for that information as its Data Fiduciary, and we process it on the business's behalf, as its Data Processor, to provide the service.
  • Business user accounts and platform operation: We determine how business-user account information and platform security records are used to operate and protect Perfect CRM.

If you are a customer of a business that uses Perfect CRM, please contact that business first about your information. You can also contact us using the details in section 13.

4. How Perfect CRM connects to Meta

Perfect CRM uses Meta's official platforms. A business connects its own Meta assets to its own Perfect CRM workspace. Perfect CRM does not ask for a Facebook password.

WhatsApp connection

A business owner or admin selects "Connect with Facebook" in Perfect CRM. This opens Meta's Embedded Signup window, where the business chooses or creates its WhatsApp Business Account and phone number and approves the permissions requested.

Perfect CRM uses the access provided by Meta to:

  • Confirm that the access covers the account selected by the business.
  • Read phone-number details, including number ID, display number and verified name.
  • Register the number for the WhatsApp Cloud API using a six-digit PIN chosen by the business. The PIN is passed to Meta and is not stored by Perfect CRM.
  • Subscribe the account to Perfect CRM webhooks so incoming messages and delivery updates reach the business's inbox.
  • Send messages that the business's users send through Perfect CRM.

Catalogue connection

A business owner or admin selects "Connect Meta catalogue" in Perfect CRM. This opens Meta's Facebook Login for Business window, where the business chooses its business portfolio and the product catalogue it wants to share, and grants Perfect CRM the catalog_management permission for that catalogue.

Perfect CRM uses this access only for the catalogue the business shared, to:

  • Confirm that the access was granted to Perfect CRM, includes catalogue permission, and comes from the business portfolio linked to the workspace.
  • List the catalogues the business shared and read the chosen catalogue's ID and name.
  • Read the catalogue's products, so the business can see them in Perfect CRM and send them to its customers in WhatsApp product messages.
  • Create, update or delete a product in that catalogue when an owner or admin of the business does so in Perfect CRM. Before creating a product, Perfect CRM checks the catalogue for an existing product with the same SKU.

Perfect CRM reads products when an owner or admin selects "Sync now", and reads a single product again after it is created or changed through Perfect CRM. Perfect CRM does not access catalogues the business did not share, and it will not connect a catalogue that is already connected to another Perfect CRM workspace.

Using a catalogue on WhatsApp

A business can link its catalogue to its WhatsApp number. Perfect CRM offers an option that asks Meta to make this link; the business can instead make the link in Meta's WhatsApp Manager and then confirm it in Perfect CRM. Confirmation actions are recorded in an audit log.

Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Perfect CRM receives information made available by Meta for the assets a business has authorised.

5. Information we process

A. Business and business-user information

  • Business name and contact details.
  • Business user names, email addresses and roles.
  • Password hashes, not readable passwords.
  • Invitation email addresses and roles.
  • WhatsApp Business Account IDs, phone number IDs and display numbers.
  • Catalogue IDs and names, the IDs of the catalogues a business shared, business portfolio IDs, and which user made the connection.
  • Connection status and connection dates.
  • Meta access tokens, protected as described in section 8.

B. Customer information

Each business's customer information is held in that business's separate workspace. This may include:

  • WhatsApp phone number and profile name supplied by Meta.
  • Names, email addresses, notes, tags and lead stages entered by business users.
  • WhatsApp message text, type, direction, time and delivery or read status.
  • Orders, customer details, delivery addresses, items, amounts, payment status, courier and tracking details, and returns.
  • Reminders and service requests recorded by the business.

Perfect CRM does not currently download or store media files such as photos, audio, video or documents sent in chats.

C. Catalogue and product information

For a connected catalogue, Perfect CRM keeps a copy of the following product information in the business's workspace:

  • Meta product ID and SKU (retailer ID).
  • Product name, description, price, currency, availability and condition.
  • Product page link, brand, and the link to the product image.
  • Meta's review status for the product, when Meta provides it.
  • The product's shipping weight, entered in Perfect CRM for courier calculations. It is not sent to Meta.
  • When the product was last read from Meta, and whether it has since been removed from the Meta catalogue.
  • Product images uploaded through Perfect CRM (see below).

Product information is business information. A product description or image could contain personal information only if the business puts it there; businesses should not do so.

Product images and file storage

Product images uploaded through Perfect CRM are stored in Vercel Blob storage in Mumbai, India. Only a business's owners and admins can upload images. Images must be JPG or PNG files of at least 500 × 500 pixels and up to 4 MB. Each file is stored under a new, neutral file name; the original file name is not kept. Images that Perfect CRM reads from a connected Meta catalogue are not copied into this storage; Perfect CRM keeps only Meta's link to them.

Uploaded images are stored with public access so that Meta and WhatsApp can display them. Anyone who has an image's direct link may be able to view it. Upload only product images you are authorised to share publicly, and never confidential, private or sensitive images, or images containing personal information.

When an owner or admin deletes a product, or replaces its image, through Perfect CRM, the uploaded image is deleted from storage, unless another product in the same workspace still uses it. Uploaded images are not deleted automatically in other cases: when a product is removed directly in Meta, when the catalogue is disconnected, or when an image is uploaded but the product is never saved. To have such images removed, email support@topinformer.in. Meta keeps its own copies of catalogue images under its own policies; removing an image from Perfect CRM does not remove it from Meta.

D. Security and audit information

  • Session identifiers and session timestamps.
  • IP addresses and browser user-agent information.
  • Sign-in and security events.
  • Audit records of sensitive actions, including Meta connections and disconnections, product creation, updates and deletions, and catalogue-link confirmations.

Audit records identify who performed an action and which business was involved. They do not contain access tokens, PINs or passwords.

E. Payments

Perfect CRM does not currently process payments. If payment features are enabled in future, this policy will be updated first.

6. Why we process information

We process information to:

  • Provide the WhatsApp inbox, contacts, orders and catalogue features.
  • Show a business's catalogue products in its workspace, include them in WhatsApp product messages the business sends, and turn WhatsApp catalogue orders into orders in the workspace.
  • Create, update and delete products in a business's Meta catalogue when the business's owner or admin asks Perfect CRM to do so.
  • Connect and maintain each business's authorised Meta assets.
  • Keep each business's workspace separate.
  • Authenticate users and control access.
  • Protect the platform, investigate security issues and maintain audit records.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell personal information or catalogue data. We do not use customer information or catalogue data for advertising or to train AI models, and we do not share one business's catalogue or customer information with another business.

8. How we protect information

  • Meta access tokens are encrypted before storage using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.
  • Encryption keys are stored separately from the database.
  • Tokens are used on the server only for the business that granted access and are never shown in the application. Only the last four characters of a token are kept unencrypted, to help identify it.
  • Passwords are stored as salted one-way scrypt hashes.
  • Setup and invitation links are stored as hashes.
  • HTTPS is used to protect information in transit.
  • Sessions use secure HTTP-only cookies.
  • Access to business workspaces is checked against the signed-in user's business membership.
  • Encrypted database backups are maintained and test-restored.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. We will handle personal data breaches and notifications in accordance with applicable law.

9. Where information is stored

Information is stored and processed using the infrastructure providers listed in section 7, and in the earlier database copies described in section 10. Current infrastructure includes services in India and Singapore. Providers may also process information in other locations under their own service arrangements.

10. How long information is kept

  • Workspace data is retained while the business account is active or until the business deletes the information.
  • Perfect CRM does not currently automatically delete conversation data after a fixed period.
  • Deleting a contact removes its conversations, messages and orders from the live database. Linked reminders and service requests are retained but become unlinked.
  • The copy of catalogue products is kept while the business's workspace is active. A product deleted through Perfect CRM is removed from the Meta catalogue; its record in Perfect CRM is kept, hidden and marked as removed, because past orders may refer to it. A product removed directly in Meta is hidden and marked as removed in the same way at the next sync. These records are deleted when the business asks us to delete them or to close its workspace.
  • Disconnecting a catalogue immediately erases its stored catalogue access token and the connection's catalogue details. Synced products remain in the workspace until deleted.
  • Security and audit records are kept to protect the platform and to show who changed what. They are not currently deleted automatically.
  • Deleted information may remain in encrypted backup files for up to 14 days and in database point-in-time history for up to 6 hours. Earlier copies of the database, made between 13 and 22 November 2026 while testing updates, rehearsing changes and testing backup recovery, are also kept. They are stored in our database provider's account (Neon, Singapore) and on the operator's computer, are accessible only to the operator, are not used to provide the service, and will be deleted when no longer needed. Information deleted from the live database may remain in these copies until they are deleted.

11. Disconnecting and revoking access

Catalogue

A business owner or admin can disconnect a Meta catalogue through Perfect CRM. The stored catalogue access token is erased immediately and Perfect CRM stops accessing the catalogue. Products already synced remain in the workspace until deleted. A business can also remove Perfect CRM's access to its catalogue in Meta Business Settings.

WhatsApp

Perfect CRM does not currently provide a self-service button to disconnect a WhatsApp account.

A business can remove Perfect CRM's access through Meta Business Settings or WhatsApp Manager and contact us to request removal of the stored WhatsApp connection and token.

Removing access in Meta does not automatically delete information already stored in Perfect CRM.
