Perfect CRM Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. About this policy

Perfect CRM is an internal customer relationship management tool used by Top Informer to manage customer conversations through one connected WhatsApp Business number. It is not currently offered as a self-service CRM for other businesses to connect their own WhatsApp numbers.

This policy describes the information processed when authorized Top Informer personnel use Perfect CRM.

2. Information processed

Depending on customer interactions and CRM use, information may include:

  • Customer WhatsApp phone numbers, profile names when available, and contact details entered by authorized personnel.
  • Incoming and outgoing WhatsApp messages, message timestamps, conversation history, and delivery or read statuses when provided by the platform.
  • WhatsApp Business Account and phone number identifiers, and related message or template metadata.
  • CRM account information for authorized personnel and technical logs needed for security and troubleshooting.

We do not ask customers to provide passwords or WhatsApp access tokens through the CRM.

3. How information is used

Information is used to receive and reply to customer enquiries, keep relevant conversation history, organize customer contacts, support customer service and business operations, maintain application security, troubleshoot errors, and meet legal obligations.

4. WhatsApp and Meta

Perfect CRM uses the WhatsApp Business Platform and related Meta services to exchange messages and relevant account or delivery information. WhatsApp and Meta process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Read Meta's Privacy Policy.

5. Who may access information

Access is limited to authorized Top Informer personnel who need the information for customer service or administration, and service providers that support hosting, database, security, or messaging functionality. Information may also be disclosed where required by law. We do not sell customer personal information.

6. Storage and security

CRM records are stored using the application's configured hosting and database services. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are intended to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse; however, no online system can guarantee absolute security. Access credentials and API tokens should be kept confidential.

7. Retention and deletion requests

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, legitimate business operations, security, and legal obligations. To request access to or deletion of personal information held by Perfect CRM, email support@topinformer.in with the subject “Perfect CRM - Data Request”. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

See our Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Customer communications

Top Informer is responsible for using customer information lawfully and following applicable WhatsApp and Meta policies, including applicable rules for messaging customers. Customers should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information through messaging channels.

9. Third-party services

Perfect CRM depends on third-party services, including Meta/WhatsApp and hosting or database providers. Their services may be governed by separate terms and privacy policies.

10. Children's privacy

Perfect CRM is an internal business tool and is not designed to target children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our data practices or the application change. The updated version and date will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data access and deletion requests, contact support@topinformer.in.
