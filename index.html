Perfect CRM Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Perfect CRM is a customer relationship management platform designed to help businesses manage contacts, conversations, and communications through supported integrations, including the WhatsApp Business Platform.

This policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared, and deleted when the platform is used.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, information may include:

  • Account details such as names, email addresses, and business names.
  • Customer details such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, tags, and notes entered by authorized users.
  • Messages, timestamps, conversation records, and message delivery statuses.
  • WhatsApp Business Account identifiers, phone number identifiers, and message template information.
  • Technical logs and diagnostic information needed to maintain security and reliability.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to provide CRM functionality, manage contacts and conversations, send and receive authorized messages, operate workflows and reminders, provide support, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and comply with legal obligations.

4. WhatsApp and Meta Integration

When a business connects a WhatsApp account, Perfect CRM may exchange relevant information with Meta and WhatsApp to provide messaging and related features. Use of those services is also subject to their applicable terms and policies.

Read Meta's Privacy Policy at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with Meta and WhatsApp, hosting and technical service providers, authorized users within the connected business, or authorities where required by law. Information is not sold as part of the intended operation of Perfect CRM.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are intended to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their passwords, access tokens, and account credentials.

7. Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the service, legitimate operational needs, and legal obligations. To request access to or deletion of information associated with Perfect CRM, contact the support address below. Requests will be reviewed subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

8. Business User Responsibilities

Businesses using Perfect CRM are responsible for having the necessary rights and permissions to upload customer information and contact customers. They must comply with applicable privacy laws and WhatsApp messaging policies.

9. Third-Party Services

Some features may depend on third-party services with separate privacy policies and terms. Users should review the policies of services they connect.

10. Children's Privacy

Perfect CRM is intended for business use and is not designed to target children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the platform or its practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
